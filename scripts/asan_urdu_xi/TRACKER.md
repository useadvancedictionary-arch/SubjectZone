## Notes and printed defects

- **L18 has no exercise in this printing.** pp. 66–67 carry the essay
  "اردو کی آخری کتاب" (with the three quoted textbook passages) and p. 68 ends
  with اشارے 1–2 only. Its part file is therefore an empty array and the chapter
  shows 0 solved items; nothing was invented.
- **L14** exercise begins at item 4 on p. 55 (items 1–3 are not printed in this
  edition); item 4 is printed as a declarative (reads like an اشارہ) and was
  answered as a وضاحت.
- **L21** قواعد-2 is printed as three sentences to explain; the text prints
  "دھانی" (for دھرتی/سرزمین) and "ہیرونی" (for ہندوی). Both kept verbatim and
  glossed in the explanation.
- **L23** page 84 is misnumbered ۸۳ in the print; its مشق 4–6 and قواعد box are
  on printed 84 and are keyed as bookPage 84.
- **L24** prints "خانواہ" for خانقاہ; kept and glossed.
- **L22** prints "راہ وار" in the poem for راہوار; kept and glossed.
- Other noted print slips: L6 "گرگ آتشنی/آتشی", L7 "غرب" (for غریب/غربت),
  L8 (الف)-3 and L13 قواعد-1 printed as statements, L12 "پچے … دیکھا رہے",
  L12 Q7 "روز نارویا", L12 Q8 "کوا یک", L13 Q3 "کھلے", L19 "بجکل سلیمان",
  L20 "گل نے" (for دلہن), L21 "نصفہ" (for نصف).

## Conventions

- Item keys: `id, lesson, section, sectionCode ('L01'..'L24'), type
  ('sq'|'mcq'), number, question, answer, explanation, bookPage, options,
  optionsRaw, correctAnswer`. Builder adds `chapter`, `pdfPage`.
- IDs: `asan_xi_l01_q1_1`, `asan_xi_l08_q3`, `asan_xi_l23_g4`.
- `section` is `مشق` for the main exercise, `قواعد` for the grammar box,
  `نوادر` for a نوادر task box (L15, L17).
- `number` mirrors the print: `(الف)-1`, `قواعد-2(ب)`, `(د)-الف`, or plain `1`.
- Vocabulary lists → one item per word; sentence-making lists → one item that
  gives a sentence for every listed word.
- Questions are copied verbatim; printed defects are kept and explained.
